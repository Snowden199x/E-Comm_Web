# Progress — 4 October 2026

- Redesigned the Buyer shell, Dashboard, and Cart page from the owner's mockups, then revised the direction: the Buyer sidebar was removed in favor of a purple header. The header has the Vendo logo, product search, Messages (`message-icon.svg`), Cart, Notifications, and an account menu (My Orders, Account Management, Logout). A category strip below it opens a Shein-style subcategory menu with round tiles for all 14 categories and their 83 subcategories. The notification bell keeps its existing polling and dropdown logic. The Buyer side now uses a compact marketplace-style scale (1200px container, 13–14px text) with smooth transitions.

- Dashboard: admin announcement banner (crossfades when there are several), scrollable Categories row, and a "Recommended for You" grid of slimmer shared product cards (six columns on wide screens). Every card has an "Add to Cart" button; products with colors or sizes open a quick-select sheet first, and the cart badge and a toast update in place through the existing Alpine AJAX plugin.

- Cart page: restyled to the mockup in Vendo colors (select-all header, store groups, quantity stepper, trash button, sticky Order Summary with Cash on Delivery and Proceed to Checkout, optional "You May Also Like"). Selection, quantity, and checkout behavior are unchanged; on a first visit all items start selected, and the shipping line reads "Calculated at checkout" because checkout records a fee of 0.

- Files: `components/buyer/layout.blade.php`, `buyer/dashboard.blade.php`, `buyer/cart.blade.php`, `buyer/partials/product-card.blade.php`, `buyer/partials/quick-add.blade.php`, `css/buyer/layout.css`, `public/assets/css/cart.css`, and `vite.config.js` (one new input, `layout.css`). Every Buyer page that uses `<x-buyer.layout>` now gets the new header and Poppins; their page bodies are unchanged until restyled. Owner-supplied assets: SVG icons in `public/assets/icons/buyer/`, category images in `public/images/buyer/categories/`, subcategory images in `public/images/buyer/subcategories/<category>/`, and an optional text-free banner at `public/images/buyer/announcement-banner.png`; missing images fall back to a grey circle, a colored letter tile, or a CSS gradient.

- Docs: added the new Buyer Dashboard spec and updated the Cart, Browse Shop, and Product Search specs, the Buyer domain page, and the domain status table.

- Known gaps (backend, not yet done): pass published Buyer-audience announcements to the dashboard (scheduled ones never auto-publish); take 18 products; eager-load seller details and add rating and sold aggregates and an optional original-price column; pass `$alsoLike` to the cart; make the product list's category filter include subcategories. No controller, route, model, or schema changes were made. No tests, build, or browser walkthrough were run; owner verification is pending (run `npm run build` or `npm run dev`).
