# Progress log: 29 October 2026

## COD cash flow, step 8: Seller earnings, Buyer notice, per-order commission

**What changed**

- **Seller Earnings page** (`seller.earnings.index`, `Seller\EarningsController`, `seller/earnings/index.blade.php`, sidebar item). Per delivered order: item price, commission at the rate saved on the order, and net earnings; totals for Pending and Credited; filters for search, status and delivered dates. Nothing new is stored. Orders placed before cash tracking are listed as "Earlier order" and not counted as Pending or Credited.
- **Automatic credit.** `CashRemittance::RECEIVED_MONEY_STATUS` for `company_to_vendo` is now `credited_to_seller`, so when the Admin confirms a Company batch (or resolves its discrepancy) the orders become credited in the same transaction. `CashRemittanceService` also sends each Seller one `earnings_credited` notification with the net amount. Orders already at `remitted_to_vendo` count as credited.
- **Order model:** `seller_commission`, `seller_net_earnings` and `seller_credited` accessors; `SELLER_CREDITED_MONEY_STATUSES`.
- **Per-order commission in Admin.** `CommissionSetting::currentRate()` now returns the Admin's seller commission setting. New `commissionSql()` and `salesAndCommission()` give each order its own saved rate (10% when none). The Admin Commission page and Reports (daily, monthly, per seller, charts) use them. Captions in those views and the PDF now say the rates are per order.
- **Buyer notice.** Checkout, order tracking and the order list show "Pay ₱X on delivery"; delivered orders show "Paid ₱X in cash on delivery".
- **Docs:** new Seller earnings spec; updated Admin commission and delivery-fee specs, checkout-order spec, design doc, domain status and README.

**Why:** finishes the Seller and Buyer sides of the COD design and makes the Admin figures consistent with the per-order rates saved at checkout.

## Station Hub pickup assignments expire after one hour

- A pickup Rider assigned from the Station Hub Pickup Requests / Task Assignment screen could hold a pickup forever: the parcel stayed pointed at a Rider who never scanned, so the hub had no clear way to hand it to someone else.
- Added `app/Services/StationPickupExpiry.php`. It releases a `pickup` task still in `assigned` 60 minutes after `assigned_at`: the task becomes `expired` with `No pickup scan within 60 minutes of assignment.`, `orders.courier_id` is cleared when it still points at that Rider, and the Rider and the staff member who assigned are notified. A parcel that is no longer `ready_for_pickup` loses the stale task without asking for a new Rider. Tasks the Rider already started, and assignments inside the window, are untouched.
- `StationOperationsController::index` and `::assign` run the sweep for their station before reading or assigning, and the Task list now also shows `expired` rows with the reason. There is no scheduler in this project, and the reassignment screen is the only place a new assignment can happen, so a lazy sweep covers the requirement without a cron entry.
- No migration: `rider_tasks.status` is already a string column. A Rider who scans after expiry gets the existing 403, because the order no longer points at them.
- Added `tests/Feature/StationPickupExpiryTest.php` (3 tests, 16 assertions). Verified in tinker and over the real HTTP route as well: a task assigned 125 minutes earlier expired and produced both notifications; a task assigned minutes ago survived; an `in_progress` task survived; a cancelled parcel expired silently with zero notifications. Order 10's assignment was restored to its original `assigned_at` afterwards. Full suite: 34 passed, 5 failed - the 5 are the pre-existing `GD extension is not installed` failures in the registration tests, unrelated to this change. Owner UI verification remains.
