# Progress — 26 September 2026

Policy previews now preserve numbering, bullets, headings and paragraph spacing in Admin and in Buyer/Seller notification dialogs. A shared renderer limits output to supported formatting and discards scripts and arbitrary attributes. Existing policy content does not need to be re-entered.

Buyer and Seller Account Management now include a Policies section, with a shortcut at the top of the page. Each account sees its role policy and the Prohibited Item Policy when content is available. Rows display the saved version and update date. The policy opens in a responsive dialog with a fixed heading, scrollable content and close controls.

The Logistics dashboard now links to Account Management. This new page shows the center's registered profile and the same Policies section. Its route requires authentication; the controller restricts access to approved, active logistics-center accounts. Policy access does not depend on notification history and does not record policy consent.
