# Vendo Privacy Policy

**Website:** vendo-ph.app · **Effective date:** 16 November 2026

Vendo processes personal data to operate its marketplace, review accounts, handle orders and delivery assignments, process returns and support requests, and provide customer care. This Policy covers the Vendo website, the Buyer app, the Rider app, and Transport Driver features. Vendo aims to process personal data in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

## 1. Information Vendo collects

- **Account and contact details:** name, email address, phone number, date of birth, password, account role, profile photo, verification and approval status, and account status.
- **Address and service area:** province, city or municipality, barangay, street or delivery details, saved delivery addresses, map pins, preferred Logistics Company, and the hub associated with a Seller, Rider, or Transport Driver when applicable.
- **Registration documents:** Buyer identity documents; Seller identity and business documents; Logistics Company registration documents; Rider identity, driver's license, vehicle, and OR/CR images; Transport Driver identity, license, and vehicle details.
- **Commerce and delivery records:** listings, cart and order items, vouchers, cash-on-delivery amount, shipping address and pin, tracking number, status and scan history, delivery assignments, delivery proof such as recipient name and photo, returns and refund records, reviews, complaints, support tickets, messages, and attachments.
- **Technical data:** information needed to operate sessions and API access, such as cookies, security tokens, IP address, device or browser details, app install and push-notification token, and server logs.
- **Google sign-in:** name and verified email returned by Google and information needed to verify the sign-in request. Vendo does not currently store a separate Google account ID in your profile.

Rider and Transport Driver parcel scans record their time and assigned hub or facility. The Rider app may read device location while a map screen is open so it can show the Rider's position and the assigned delivery pin. Vendo does not store a continuous GPS trail. A Buyer may optionally attach a map pin or current device location to an order; that pin is stored with the order and shown to the assigned Rider. Some address and map screens use Geoapify and OpenStreetMap map data.

## 2. Why Vendo uses this information

Vendo uses account and document details to create and review accounts, verify email, match Riders and Drivers to the appropriate hub, and control access to assigned work. It uses order, address, assignment, scan, delivery-proof, return, and message information to fulfill cash-on-delivery orders, show order progress, arrange returns and pickups, communicate with participants, handle complaints and support tickets, and prepare sales and commission records. Technical data supports sign-in, push delivery, fraud prevention, service operation, and troubleshooting.

Vendo sends Rider push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging when the Rider app registers a device token and the Rider allows notifications. Buyer, Seller, Admin, and Transport Driver push notifications are not sent at this time. If permission is denied, the Rider app can still show notifications through its in-app polling fallback.

## 3. Who receives information

Vendo shares information within an order or application only as needed for the relevant task: Sellers receive Buyer order and delivery details; approved Logistics Companies and their authorized hub staff see parcels, Rider and Driver applications, delivery work, and settlement records for their scope; assigned Riders and Transport Drivers receive the pickup, delivery, trip, or return details needed for their work; and authorized Vendo administrators review registrations, reports, complaints, support matters, and account requests. Buyers and Sellers can see their own order timeline and relevant messages.

Service providers may process information to provide hosting, network protection, email delivery, Google sign-in, Geoapify address search and map tiles, OpenStreetMap map data, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Vendo may disclose information to authorities when legally required. Vendo does not sell personal data.

## 4. Storage and protection

Vendo uses account access controls, password hashing for regular login passwords, email verification, and encrypted connections for the deployed service. Buyer, Seller, Logistics Company, Rider, and Transport Driver verification uploads are stored outside the website's public file directory and are accessed by authorized reviewers. Delivery-proof photos, support-ticket attachments, complaint evidence, and similar private files are also kept on private storage and served only to authorized users. Push-device tokens are stored with the account they belong to and are removed when the app unregisters them or the provider reports them invalid. No online service can promise absolute security.

## 5. How long information is kept

Vendo keeps account and transaction information while needed to operate accounts, complete orders, resolve returns, complaints, or disputes, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal duties. Vendo does not yet apply a fixed automatic deletion period to every data category. Approving an account-deletion request deactivates access but keeps order, product, message, and other records that may still be needed. Support-ticket attachments, delivery proof, and complaint evidence may remain while the related matter is open or while retention is required. You may ask about deletion by emailing the address below; outstanding orders and legal obligations may affect what can be deleted and when.

## 6. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to be informed about processing, access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking, request portability where applicable, and seek damages or file a complaint. A Buyer or Seller can also submit an account-deletion request from the account's privacy or security settings. Contact Vendo at vendoapp.official@gmail.com to exercise these rights. You may also contact the [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-subject-rights/).

## 7. Cookies and app permissions

The website uses session and security cookies to keep accounts signed in and protect forms. Disabling them may prevent account features from working. The Rider app uses camera access for parcel barcode or QR scans and may use the camera for delivery proof. The app may request photo or file access when you upload registration documents, delivery proof, complaint evidence, or support attachments. Rider map screens may request device location; parcel scan records do not contain continuous location data. Push notifications require the operating-system notification permission. You can deny or later revoke a permission in your device settings; some features will then be unavailable.

## 8. Minors

Seller, Logistics Company, Province Hub and Station Hub manager, Rider, and Transport Driver accounts are for adults. A minor may use a Buyer account only with the knowledge, consent, and guidance of a parent or legal guardian. A parent or guardian may contact vendoapp.official@gmail.com about a minor's account or privacy rights.

## 9. Changes and contact

Vendo may update this Policy and announce material changes through the platform or by email where required. The current version and effective date are available on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact vendoapp.official@gmail.com.
