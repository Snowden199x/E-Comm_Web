# Vendo Privacy Policy

**Website:** vendo-ph.app  
**Effective date:** 28 September 2026

Vendo processes personal data to operate its marketplace, review accounts, handle orders and delivery assignments, and provide support. This Policy covers the Vendo website and the Buyer and Rider mobile apps. Vendo aims to process personal data in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

## 1. Information Vendo collects

- **Account and contact details:** name, email address, phone number, date of birth, password, account role, verification and approval status.
- **Address and service area:** province, city or municipality, barangay, street or delivery details, and the Main Hub associated with a Seller or Rider when applicable.
- **Registration documents:** Buyer identity documents; Seller identity and business documents; Logistics Center registration documents; Rider identity, driver's license, vehicle, and OR/CR images.
- **Commerce and delivery records:** listings, cart and order items, cash-on-delivery amount, shipping address, tracking number, status and scan history, delivery assignments, reviews, complaints, and support or marketplace messages and attachments.
- **Technical data:** information needed to operate sessions and API access, such as cookies, security tokens, IP address, device or browser details, and server logs.
- **Google sign-in:** name and verified email returned by Google and information needed to verify the sign-in request. Vendo does not currently store a separate Google account ID in your profile.

Rider parcel scans record their time and assigned hub; they do not create a continuous GPS trail. Some Rider app map screens may ask to use device location to display a map or delivery details. If Vendo later adds live location tracking, this Policy and the relevant app permission notice will be updated.

## 2. Why Vendo uses this information

Vendo uses account and document details to create and review accounts, verify email, match Riders to a Main Hub, and control access to assigned work. It uses order, address, assignment, scan, and message information to fulfill cash-on-delivery orders, show order progress, communicate with participants, handle complaints, and prepare sales and commission reports. Technical data supports sign-in, fraud prevention, service operation, and troubleshooting.

## 3. Who receives information

Vendo shares information within an order or application only as needed for the relevant task: Sellers receive Buyer order and delivery details; approved Logistics Centers see parcels and Rider applications for their center; assigned Riders receive the pickup or delivery details needed for their work; and authorized Vendo administrators review registrations, reports, complaints, and support matters. Buyers and Sellers can see their own order timeline and relevant messages.

Service providers may process information to provide hosting, network protection, email delivery, and Google sign-in. Vendo may disclose information to authorities when legally required. Vendo does not sell personal data.

## 4. Storage and protection

Vendo uses account access controls, password hashing for regular login passwords, email verification, and encrypted connections for the deployed service. Buyer, Seller, Logistics Center, and Rider verification uploads are stored outside the website's public file directory and are accessed by authorized reviewers. No online service can promise absolute security.

## 5. How long information is kept

Vendo keeps account and transaction information while needed to operate accounts, complete orders, resolve complaints or disputes, and meet applicable legal duties. Vendo does not yet apply a fixed automatic deletion period to every data category. You may request account closure or ask about deletion by emailing the address below; outstanding orders and legal obligations may affect what can be deleted and when.

## 6. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to be informed about processing, access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking, request portability where applicable, and seek damages or file a complaint. Contact Vendo at vendoapp.official@gmail.com to exercise these rights. You may also contact the [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-subject-rights/).

## 7. Cookies and app permissions

The website uses session and security cookies to keep accounts signed in and protect forms. Disabling them may prevent account features from working. The Rider app uses camera access for parcel barcode or QR scans. Rider map screens may request device location; parcel scan records do not contain continuous location data.

## 8. Minors

Seller, Logistics Center, and Rider accounts are for adults. A minor may use a Buyer account only with the knowledge, consent, and guidance of a parent or legal guardian. A parent or guardian may contact vendoapp.official@gmail.com about a minor's account or privacy rights.

## 9. Changes and contact

Vendo may update this Policy and announce material changes through the platform or by email where required. For privacy questions or requests, contact vendoapp.official@gmail.com.
