# Province Hub and Company Cash Remittances

**Status:** Web workflow written in code; migrations and owner verification pending. Continues the [Station Hub cash collections](../station-cash-collections/spec.md) and the [COD cash and delivery fee design](../../../design/cod-cash-and-delivery-fee-distribution.md).

No real money moves. Each step records that the receiver confirmed, or disputed, the cash a sender reports.

**Province Hub — Cash Remittances** (`GET /logistics/portal/province/cash`, `lgp.province.cash`): shows cash awaiting confirmation, verified cash ready to send, open discrepancies and cash sent and awaiting the Company, then the `station_to_province` batches addressed to this Province Hub. *Confirm receipt* (`POST .../cash/remittances/{remittance}/receive`) takes the amount received. A matching amount sets the batch `received` and moves its orders from `remitted_to_station` to `remitted_to_province`. A different amount requires a note, sets the batch `discrepancy` and holds its orders in `discrepancy`. *Resolve* (`PATCH .../resolve`) needs a note, sets the batch `resolved` and returns its orders to `remitted_to_province`. *Send to Company* (`POST .../cash/remit`) batches every `remitted_to_province` order whose `station_to_province` batch was received or resolved at this Province Hub and that is not yet in a `province_to_company` batch into one `pending` batch for the Company.

**Company — Cash Remittances** (`GET /logistics/portal/company/cash`, `lgp.company.cash`): the same confirm and resolve flow for `province_to_company` batches (orders move to `received_by_company`), and a card for the delivery fee split on cash received (Company earnings, Rider pay held for payout and Vendo's delivery commission, summed from `order_shipping_settlements` for confirmed parcels). *Send to Vendo* (`POST .../cash/remit`) creates a `pending` `company_to_vendo` batch for every confirmed order not yet sent. For each order the amount is the cash collected minus what the Company keeps (the Rider payment plus its delivery fee share from the settlement, or the whole delivery fee when no settlement exists). That equals the item price after any voucher plus Vendo's delivery commission. Vendo confirms them on the Admin Cash Remittances page; until then they stay `pending` and the orders stay `received_by_company`.

**Company — Rider Payment** (`GET/PATCH /logistics/portal/company/rider-pay`, `lgp.company.rider-pay`): sets `logistics_companies.rider_pay_per_parcel`, validated between ₱0 and the lowest delivery fee (₱45, `CheckoutShippingQuoteService::MINIMUM_FEE_CENTS`) so the fee can always cover it. The page shows a worked example of a ₱45 fee using the current Vendo delivery commission. Changes apply to new orders only because each order snapshots the rate at checkout.

Shared rules: confirming or resolving locks the batch and its orders, a batch can be confirmed once, and an order can be in at most one batch per level (unique `order_id`, `level`). Province pages act only on batches addressed to the signed-in Province Hub, and Company pages only on batches for the signed-in Company. `CashRemittanceService` holds the shared receive, resolve and query logic. The Station Hub's Rider-level receive and its own batch step stay in `StationCashController`.

Sidebar entries (`config/logistics_nav.php`): Province Business group gains **Cash Remittances**; the Company sidebar gains a **Money** group with **Cash Remittances** and **Rider Payment**.

Known gaps: shortage liability is recorded only as notes, and no step reverses a batch when an order is later returned. Parcels that settle as `review` (route mismatch) are counted with no Rider payment, so the Company keeps the full delivery fee. Vendo's receipt confirmation is on the [Admin cash remittances page](../../admin/delivery-fees-and-remittances/spec.md), Seller earnings on the [Seller earnings page](../../seller/earnings/spec.md), and the Admin rate editor on the [Admin delivery fees page](../../admin/delivery-fees-and-remittances/spec.md).
