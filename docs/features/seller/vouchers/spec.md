# Seller Vouchers

**Status:** Seller voucher management, Buyer checkout selection, and voucher notifications implemented in code; owner migration and manual verification pending.
**Reviewed:** 13 October 2026

An approved active Seller can create, list, filter, edit, pause, resume, and retire shop voucher codes. Codes are unique per Seller, 4–12 uppercase letters or digits. A voucher can be a fixed amount or at most 90% off, optionally capped, with a minimum eligible spend, overall/per-Buyer usage limits, and start/end times. Scope is all Seller products, selected Seller products, or approved Seller main categories (including their subcategories). Server validation checks ownership and the limits. Retiring a code keeps its redemption history and reserves its code.

Buyer checkout allows one voucher per Seller order, selected from matching Seller offers or entered as a code. Applying it shows a server-calculated discount before placing the order. The checkout transaction locks the voucher and selected cart rows, rechecks eligibility and usage, saves `voucher_code` and `voucher_discount` on the order, and records one redemption. `orders.total_amount` remains the gross product subtotal; payable COD is subtotal minus voucher discount plus shipping. A permitted Buyer/Seller pre-pickup cancellation voids the redemption and releases its usage count once. Past orders retain their code and discount snapshot even if the Seller edits or retires the voucher.

The Seller voucher page shows nonvoided redemption counts and discount totals. Buyer order details, Seller order/shipment/completed-order details, and the waybill show the discounted payable amount. The Admin product commission remains 10% of item price × quantity in delivered/completed orders; voucher funding and net Seller settlement still need an explicit business rule.
The Seller product list counts currently available vouchers that apply to each loaded product, including shop-wide, product-specific, and main-category codes.

Buyer checkout now lists active, unexpired shop-wide, product-scoped, and category-scoped vouchers that match selected lines for each Seller order. Buyers can choose a redeemable offer or enter a code manually; offers with a future start, unmet minimum, exhausted use limit, or reached per-Buyer limit stay visible with the reason and are disabled. Terms and scope are shown, and the existing quote and final transaction validate the discount. On first activation, prior Buyers receive one notification per Buyer/voucher linking to the shop. Seller notifications create one alert when a voucher reaches its end time or exhausts its total use limit; the bell's recent-notification polling detects it, so it appears within the next poll while a Seller page is open. Voucher notification timestamps and the deduplication table require the new migration.

There is no voucher discovery/catalog on Buyer cart or product pages, stacking, free-shipping vouchers, refund ledger, or payout accounting. The preview quote is revalidated at final checkout and can change if stock, voucher status, or usage changes before submission. Shipping remains zero pending a tariff decision. The migration marks pre-existing vouchers as already announced and already-ended active vouchers as already notified, so new-voucher and expiration alerts begin with later events. The owner must run migrations and verify the flow.
